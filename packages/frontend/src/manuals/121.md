# 121

Start at level **121**, checking out with a double or inner bull. Each attempt
allows **6 or 9 darts**, across two or three visits. Remaining score carries
between visits. Overshooting, leaving 1, or an invalid finish busts the visit,
restoring its starting remainder while consuming that visit.

Success increases your level by **Step: 1, 3 or 5**, capped at 170.
After an unsuccessful attempt, **Soft** drops one level, **Hard reset** returns
to 121, and **Safehouse** returns to the banked level. No failure drops below
121. Safehouses bank the new level every **1, 2, 3 or 5 checkouts**.

Checking out 170 wins immediately. Otherwise **Attempts per player** limits the
session: highest final level wins, with seat order breaking ties. If everyone
ends at 121, no win is recorded. Peak level alone does not decide the winner.

Cards show remaining score, level, completed attempts, visits and safehouse.
Takeout, undo, correction, restart, legs and sets work normally.
