# Around the Clock

Complete your own target sequence before the other players.

Choose **ascending**, **descending** or **random** order. Bull is last by
default; disable **Finish on bull** to stop after the numbered targets.

**Full** accepts any ring of the number. **Single** accepts both single areas;
**Outer Single**, **Double** and **Triple** require their specific zone.
Require **1, 2 or 3 hits** per target. Multipliers never skip targets.

For bull, Full accepts either ring, single modes require outer bull, and
Double/Triple require inner bull. There is no triple bull.

Three darts per visit, without bonus turns. The board highlights your target;
the card shows accuracy and progress. First to finish wins immediately.
Early ending or a round cap compares successful progress, with seat order
breaking ties. Undo, correction, restart, legs and sets work normally.
