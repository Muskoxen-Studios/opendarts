# Bob's 27

A solo doubles routine. Start with **27 points** and throw three darts at D1,
then D2, continuing through D20. **Finish on bull** adds inner bull as the last
target; outer bull does not count.

Every correct double adds its board value. If all three darts miss the target,
subtract that value once. For example, two D3 hits add 12; no D3 hits subtract 6.

Normal mode ends the session as soon as a completed visit leaves zero or less,
without a win. **Allow negative score** lets you complete the sequence anyway.
A positive final score wins; a non-positive score is saved without a win.

The card shows current double, accuracy, visit changes and peak score.
Exactly one player is required and late joins are unavailable. Undo reverses
penalties; correction, restart, takeout, legs and sets work normally. Early
ending or a round cap records a win only with a positive score.
