# Conquest

Expand your territory, interrupt enemy attacks, and protect your home. The last independent faction wins the leg; you do not need to own every field.

## Starting positions

Choose **2, 4, 5, 10 or 20 players**. Each starts with one home, equally spaced around the physical board, beginning at 20. Homes carry a ★ and use their owner's colour. All other territory starts neutral. Starting positions stay fixed within a leg. Late joins are unavailable; players may leave. After departures, new legs distribute remaining homes as evenly as possible.

Everyone throws three darts per turn in the original seating order. A capture takes effect immediately: remaining darts can expand from the field you just captured. Misses and outer bull have no effect. Inner bull can unlock Power of God for the uniquely last faction leader.

## Capturing and defending

A field takes **3 marks** to capture. Own an adjacent field to build an attack. Progress carries across turns, and you may attack several fields at once. Existing ownership remains until capture completes.

An enemy hitting an ordinary field clears the existing attack from anywhere on the board. If that enemy also owns a neighbour, the same dart starts their attack. Otherwise it only sabotages. Hitting friendly territory clears enemy progress on it. Allies share attacks and defense.

Losing adjacency stops further capture marks until you regain a neighbour; it does not itself erase existing progress.

## 20 or 40 territories

With **20 territories**, each number is one field. All its scoring rings affect that field. Adjacent means the physical neighbouring numbers: 20 borders 5 and 1.

With **40 territories**, every number has separate inner and outer fields. Each borders the two neighbouring numbers in the same ring, plus the other half of its own number. There are no diagonal borders or connections through bull. Homes start on the outer ring.

- Inner single and treble affect the **inner** field.
- Outer single and double affect the **outer** field.

The treble ring divides the two halves; it affects the inner field rather than forming a separate territory.

## Optional multipliers

Normally every dart gives **1 mark**, including doubles and trebles. Enable multipliers for singles = 1, doubles = 2, trebles = 3. In the 40-field board a double only builds an outer attack and a treble only builds an inner attack. Excess marks do not spill into another field.

## On home capture

**Every home-base capture transfers all territory owned by the defeated faction to the attacker**, including both rings and territory acquired from earlier recruits. The selected mode determines what happens to its players.

**Elimination:** the defeated faction's players are out, and you take all their land. Its unfinished ordinary attacks disappear.

**Conversion:** the defeated faction's players, territories and unfinished attacks join the conqueror. Everyone keeps their original place in the turn order. Members share capture counters and can defend each other's land. Captured homes become ordinary territory; the conquering faction's original home remains its capital. Capturing that capital converts the entire faction again.

The original leader of the final faction wins the leg and match honours; converted players are allies.

## Optional comeback foothold

With ordinary homes, defeated players keep taking turns. Three marks on your original home reclaim it **without adjacency**, making you independent again. In elimination mode this is your only available action until you return. In conversion mode you may keep helping your faction or attack your original home to leave it. Only your home returns to you; the current owner keeps all other land and allies.

Comeback attacks can be interrupted and defended like other ordinary attacks, including by your current faction. The leg still ends immediately when just one independent faction remains: comeback does not reopen a finished leg.

## Optional fortified homes

Homes require **9 permanent marks** instead of 3. Friendly hits cannot heal them, and remote enemy hits cannot erase their damage. Only adjacent enemies can add marks; damage remains even if the attacker loses adjacency or is defeated. Different enemy factions contribute to the same damage total; whoever lands the final mark captures the home.

Take the attacker's neighbouring territories to stop further damage. Captured fortified homes become ordinary 3-mark fields and **cannot be reclaimed as a comeback foothold**. Fortified homes override the comeback option.

## Power of God

If your independent faction owns **strictly fewer territories than every other independent faction**, you can hit the **inner bull (50)** to unlock **Power of God**. A tie for last means nobody qualifies. Converted allies and eliminated players are excluded from this ranking and cannot arm the power.

Once armed, **one hit on an enemy faction's surviving home instantly captures its base and all its territories**, from anywhere on the board. This bypasses adjacency, the normal three marks, and fortified homes' nine marks. Every field owned by the defeated faction changes to your colour, including territory acquired from earlier recruits and both rings of the split board. As with normal home captures, this territory transfer applies in both modes. The defeated players are still eliminated or recruited according to the selected mode.

The charge belongs to the leader who earned it. It survives misses, friendly hits, ordinary territory hits and turn boundaries, and is spent only on a successful instant capital capture. Charges do not stack. You may earn another charge with another inner bull while eligible.

**Catching up, tying for last, losing your home, restarting or starting a new leg removes the charge.** Becoming uniquely last again does not restore it; hit inner bull again. The normal victory rule still ends the leg immediately when one independent faction remains.

On the 40-field board, hit the actual **outer home**: its outer single or double. The inner half and treble of that number do not activate the instant capture. Former capitals and friendly homes are not instant-kill targets.

The panel announces when Power of God is available or armed. Inner bull lights up while you can unlock it; enemy capitals receive capture highlights while you are armed.

## Reading the board

Territories show their faction colour; grey is neutral. Gold borders identify fields the active player can capture. Badges show attack marks and the required total; their outline identifies the current attacker. ★ identifies a home. The faction panel lists territory counts, allegiances and home damage.

## Round limits and ending early

An optional round limit awards the leg to the independent leader with the most territories. End game uses the same ranking, with previously won sets and legs taking precedence. Converted and eliminated players rank below independent leaders; ties use seating order. Legs and sets work normally, with turn starters rotating between legs.
