# Count Up

Build the highest total over a fixed number of rounds. Play alone to practise
scoring or compete with other players.

## Scoring

Start at zero. Every dart adds its ordinary board value: singles count once,
doubles twice, trebles three times. Outer bull scores 25, inner bull scores 50,
and misses score nothing. There are no busts or checkout requirements.

## Turns and winning

Each player throws three darts per round. The default is **8 rounds** (24 darts
per player); set **Rounds per player** from 1 to 100. Everyone gets their final
turn before scores are compared. Highest total wins; tied totals go to the
earlier player in the lineup.

The scoreboard shows completed visits and your three-dart average. Remove your
darts to hand over, or use **End turn** for a shortened visit. An optional round
limit can shorten the leg. Legs and sets work as in other games.

Undo, correction and restart work normally. **End game** awards the player with
the highest total, after considering sets and legs already won.
