# Round the World

Take three darts at every target. Correct singles earn **1 mark**, doubles
**2**, triples **3**; other numbers and misses earn nothing. Advance after each
visit whether you hit or missed.

Choose **ascending**, **descending** or **random** order. **Finish on bull** is
enabled by default: outer bull earns 1 mark and inner bull 2.

Every player gets a visit at each target. Highest marks after the final visit
wins; equal totals use seat order. The live card shows target, hits, accuracy
and visit marks. Remove darts to hand over, or end a visit early with **End turn**.

Undo, correction, restart, legs and sets work normally. A round cap shortens
the sequence for everyone. **End game** compares banked sets/legs, then marks.
