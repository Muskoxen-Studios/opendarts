# Segment Training

Practise one number and zone. **Single** accepts both single areas;
**Outer Single**, **Double** and **Triple** accept only the named zone.
Wrong zones, other numbers and misses do not count as hits.

Choose **Ends after hits** to race to the hit goal, or **Ends after darts** to
give each player exactly that many darts and compare hits. The final visit can
be shorter than three darts. Ties use seat order.

In **Random** mode OpenDarts chooses a numbered single, double or triple,
keeps it after misses, and chooses again after a hit. The saved random sequence
survives undo, correction and resume.

The board highlights the accepted area. Cards track target, hits, dart count
and accuracy. Normal takeout, restart, legs and sets apply. A round cap or
**End game** compares hit counts after banked sets/legs.
